# Reltio IDE

Manage your Reltio tenant's business configuration directly from VS Code or Cursor — without leaving your editor.

---

## What it does

Reltio IDE connects to your Reltio tenant and gives you a full editing environment for your business configuration. You can browse, edit, import, and publish your business configuration, validate it as you type, and visualize the full ontology as an interactive graph.

---

## Features

**Easy Tenant Connectivity**

Connect securely to your Reltio environment in minutes using your client credentials and start modeling immediately.

**AI-Assisted Business Configuration Authoring**

Create entities, relationships, attributes, and references using natural language or guided authoring. Reltio IDE understands your model and automatically generates the required configuration and dependencies.

**Intelligent Navigation**

Browse the complete L3 configuration with easy navigation between entities, relationships, attributes, and URIs. Find definitions and references instantly.

**Smart Code Completion & Validation**

Get context-aware suggestions, auto-completion, and real-time validation while editing metadata, helping you catch issues before deployment.

**Automatic Dependency Management**

Create reference attributes without worrying about prerequisite relationship types—Reltio IDE creates the required supporting resources automatically.

**Ontology Visualization**

Visualize the complete business configuration to better understand entities, relationships, and their connections across your tenant.

**Safe Configuration Deployment**

Review changes with side-by-side comparisons before applying them to your tenant, reducing deployment risk.

**Version History & Change Tracking**

View configuration history, compare versions, and understand who changed what and when for better collaboration and governance.

## Getting started

1. Install the extension from the `.vsix` file
2. Open Cursor
3. Open the Command Palette: Cmd+Shift+P (macOS) or Ctrl+Shift+P (Windows/Linux)
4. Type: Install from VSIX
5. Select: Extensions: Install from VSIX...
6. Browse and pick your file
7. Reltio IDE should appear in your installed extensions
8. Click the Reltio icon in the activity bar
9. Click 'Connect your Reltio Tenant' and follow the setup steps
10. Your tenant configuration opens automatically once connected

---

## Requirements

- Cursor
- A Reltio tenant and valid credentials (browser OAuth or bearer token)
